About Me

I am an undergraduate majoring in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on large language model pretraining, especially model architecture and scaling laws.

  • Scaling laws. I use experiments with smaller models to predict larger models’ performance and identify promising hyperparameter settings.
  • Concept-level language modeling. I study architectures that group tokens into semantic concepts and predict future concepts to improve token-level modeling and capture long-range dependencies.
